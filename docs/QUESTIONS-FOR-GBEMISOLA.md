# A few questions for your website

Hi Gbemisola,

The new site is built. What it needs now is **you**: real details that only The Right Clean could say. Short answers are fine, voice notes are fine, and skip anything you'd rather not share.

## Your story

1. How did The Right Clean start? Was there a moment you decided to go out on your own?
2. Why medical and dental offices? How did you get into cleaning clinics?
3. What does a "proper" clean mean to you? What do you check before you leave a job?
4. What do you see other cleaners do (or skip) that bothers you?
5. Is there a job you're proud of, or a tough one you turned around? (No client names needed.)
6. Which part of Edmonton did you start in? Where do you work most now?

## How you work (these become the FAQ)

7. Do you bring your own supplies and equipment? Any products you prefer, such as eco-friendly or hospital-grade?
8. Do you clean evenings or weekends, or after business hours for offices and clinics?
9. Do you take regular weekly or monthly contracts, one-off jobs, or both?
10. Do you need to see the space before quoting, or can you quote from photos and a description?
11. How fast do you usually get back with a quote?
12. What happens if a client says something was missed?
13. For move-outs, do you clean inside the oven and fridge?
14. For clinics, is there anything you do differently from an office?
15. How many people are on the team right now? Is it fine to say so on the site?
16. Insurance: you said yes to insured, WCB and bonded. Can we list all three?

## Proof

17. **Reviews:** 2–3 short quotes from happy clients (first name + last initial), or screenshots of Facebook messages or reviews you're allowed to use.
18. **Photos:** even phone photos help a lot:
    - you at work
    - the team, uniforms or the vehicle
    - your equipment
    - before/after shots from real jobs (no client faces or addresses)
19. Any businesses or clinics you clean for that would be happy to be named?
20. The original logo file, if you have it.

## Quick checks

21. Are you closed on Saturdays and Sundays? The site currently says so.
22. How quickly do you usually reply to a quote request? The site doesn't say yet.
23. Is it OK that the site says "If something isn't right, call or text me"?

Thank you!
Samuel
